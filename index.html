Low Stock

What needs reordering, worst first. Every product has a minimum —worked out automatically from what goes out, or one you set (the Auto | Manual pill). When a balance drops below it, the product shows here and a banner pops up across the top of every page, on every device using the app.

Every item on this line, its balance, its minimum, and how many days that balance covers. Set minimums rebuilds them from real sales —the 90th percentile of rolling 7-day demand, enough for a bad week in 9 weeks out of 10.

Sales Import

Drop the SI_Item export (or any sheet with a product and a quantity column). The system reads INVOICEDATE / MATERIAL / UOM / TOTALQTY, skips 合计小计 subtotal rows, and combines invoice lines per item. Every line is remembered by Bill No, so re-importing the same file skips duplicates and a revised invoice applies only the difference. Pack size must match exactly —a line whose UOM is not one you stock is reported and left out rather than deducted from the wrong pack. If the file also has a total amount column (TOTALAMT / AMOUNT / 金额), that's captured too —see the Sales station for revenue by item.

Receive Stock

Stock arriving at this warehouse. Drop YonYou's purchase export (PI_Item), or key a delivery in by hand. The export covers every warehouse —only products on this warehouse's item list are taken in, the rest are skipped. Quantities are read in the stock unit (TOTALSQTY / SUOM), so cartons become packets automatically. Every line is remembered by its bill number: importing the same file again adds nothing, and a revised bill adds only the difference.

Item list

Upload a sheet with one row per item. Recognised columns: name / 名称, uom / 单位, stock / 库存 / physical qty, min / 最低, code, category, storage / rack position, loose qty. Matching names update in place, new names are added. On a warehouse department, any rack position found in the sheet is added to the Racks board automatically, and it also reads zone (Dry / Chiller / Freezer), pkts per ctn, ctn qty + loose pkt qty —blank cells leave what's there alone, so Download current list, fill in pack sizes, and drop it back in.

Line settings

Capacity drives the load meter on the cook schedule. The cover target is what Set minimums on the stock page uses to rebuild every minimum from real usage.

Stocktake report

Once the count is done —on Racks, Count or Stocktake— this is the record of it: every product's balance as it stands now, rack by rack and zone by zone, with what the count changed. Print it to sign off, or download it for Excel.
